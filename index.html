CHEM-411: Basic Concepts of Inorganic Chemistry

An advanced exploration of theoretical frameworks in inorganic chemistry, covering acid-base systems, non-aqueous chemistry, symmetry group theory, and nuclear transformations.

Total Marks: 100[cite: 2]
Credits: 4[cite: 2]
Units: 4 Comprehensive Modules
Explore Syllabus Recommended Reading

Course Syllabus Units

Select any unit card or expand topics to review core academic contents.

UNIT 01[cite: 2]

Acid-Base Concepts

Fundamental acid-base systems, electron-donor-acceptor theories, and complex stability paradigms[cite: 2].

  • Arrhenius concept (Water ion system)[cite: 2]
  • Brønsted-Lowry theory (Proton-donor-acceptor)[cite: 2]
  • General theory of solvent system[cite: 2]
  • Lewis concept (Electron-donor-acceptor)[cite: 2]
  • Hard-Soft Acid-Base (HSAB) concept & complex stability[cite: 2]
  • Usanovich concept (Positive-negative system)[cite: 2]
UNIT 02[cite: 2]

Non-aqueous Solvents

Classification, properties, and chemical reactions across diverse ionizing solvent systems[cite: 2].

  • Classification & general properties of ionizing solvents[cite: 2]
  • Liquid Ammonia & Liquid Sulfur Dioxide[cite: 2]
  • Liquid Dinitrogen Tetra-oxide & Hydrogen Fluoride[cite: 2]
  • Hydrogen Sulfide, Hydrogen Cyanide & Acetic Acid[cite: 2]
  • Liquid Bromine Trifluoride & Oxyhalides[cite: 2]
UNIT 03[cite: 2]

Chemical Applications of Group Theory

Molecular symmetry, point groups, matrix representations, and character table theorems[cite: 2].

  • Symmetry elements, operations & multiplication[cite: 2]
  • Symmetry point groups & high-symmetry groups[cite: 2]
  • Molecular dissymmetry and optical activity[cite: 2]
  • Matrix representations, reducible/irreducible reps[cite: 2]
  • Great Orthogonality Theorem & Character Tables[cite: 2]
UNIT 04[cite: 2]

Nuclear Chemistry

Nuclear stability models, fission/fusion dynamics, tracer isotopes, and radiopharmaceuticals[cite: 2].

  • Nuclear structure models and stability[cite: 2]
  • Nuclear reactions: Fission and fusion[cite: 2]
  • Radioactivity detection & radioisotope tracers[cite: 2]
  • Isotope effects, dilution techniques & radiometric titrations[cite: 2]
  • Radiopharmaceuticals, radioimmunoassay & hot atom chemistry[cite: 2]
